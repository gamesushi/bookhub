# Piecing Together [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]'s 1986 Annual Meeting
:::
:::lang chs
# 整合[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司 1986 年年会
:::

:::lang eng

CNBC’s invaluable [archive](https://buffett.cnbc.com/annual-meetings/) of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] annual meeting videos and transcripts only dates back to 1994. Meaning that decades’ worth of Q&A sessions with [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] and [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie Munger]] remain shrouded in the mists of time.

:::

:::lang eng

CNBC

:::

:::lang chs

宝贵的 [archive](https://buffett.cnbc.com/annual-meetings/) [[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司年会视频和文字记录只能追溯到 1994 年。这意味着，数十年来沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]和查理-[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]的问答会谈仍然深藏在时间的迷雾中。

:::

:::lang eng

You will occasionally stumble upon a fragmentary account about one of the early [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] meetings, whether it’s in the pages of the _Omaha World-Herald_, _Outstanding Investor Digest_, or _University of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] Hathaway_ (by Daniel Pecaut and Corey Wrenn). These typically include a few partial quotes and other highlights from [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]’s remarks — but nothing like the comprehensive records of the post-1993 meetings.

:::

:::lang chs

你偶尔会在《奥马哈世界先驱报》、《杰出投资者文摘》（Outstanding Investor Digest）或《波克夏大學 : [[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]與窮查理30年的投資備忘錄》[注：台版]（University of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]）（作者丹尼尔-佩考特（Daniel Pecaut）和科里-沃伦（Corey Wrenn））的页面上，偶然发现有关[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]早期会议的零星报道。这些内容通常包括[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]发言的部分引语和其他要点，但与 1993 年后会议的全面记录完全不同。

:::

:::lang eng

So, today, I’ll do my best to piece together various accounts — from the three aforementioned sources — about [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s annual meeting in 1986. I’ve compared several different reports from the meeting and organized the commentary by topic.

:::

:::lang chs

因此，今天，我将尽我所能，从上述三个来源中整理出有关[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司 1986 年年会的各种报道。我比较了会议的几份不同报告，并按主题整理了实况。

:::

:::lang eng

This cannot claim to be the definitive study of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s 1986 meeting — there are, no doubt, holes big enough to drive a truck through — but it’s my best effort to compile all of the available information about the wit and wisdom on display that day.

:::

:::lang chs

这不能算是对1986年[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]会议的最终定论--毋庸置疑，其中定有诸多疏漏--但这是我尽最大努力整理出的关于当天所展示的机智和智慧的所有可用信息。

:::

:::lang eng

On May 20, 1986, approximately 500 [[股东|shareholders]] filed into the Witherspoon Concert Hall at Omaha’s Joslyn Art Museum for the [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] annual meeting.

:::

:::lang chs

1986 年 5 月 20 日，大约 500 名股东来到奥马哈乔斯林艺术博物馆的威瑟斯彭音乐厅，参加[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司的年会。

:::

:::lang eng

That Tuesday morning, [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] and [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie Munger]] sped through the formal proceedings in a matter of minutes — before opening the floor to questions from [[股东|shareholders]] for the next two-and-a-half hours.

:::

:::lang chs

星期二上午，沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]和查理-[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]在短短几分钟内就完成了正式流程，然后在接下来的两个半小时里接受股东提问。

:::

:::lang eng

Below is everything that I could find from this (very) early episode of the Warren & [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie]] Show…

:::

:::lang chs

以下是我能从这期（非常）早期的沃伦与查理问答中找到的所有内容......

# On inflation

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]

:::

:::lang eng

1.“\[Inflation\] is a political phenomenon, not an economic one. If you print lots of tickets, ticket prices decline. If you print lots of dollars, the value of each dollar goes down. Eventually, that has to happen. I see no evidence of any great self-restraint that will change our propensity to print money.”

:::

:::lang chs

1.[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]是一种政治现象，而不是经济现象。如果你印了很多票，票价就会下降。如果你大量印制美元，每美元的价值就会下降。最终，这必然会发生。我没有看到任何证据表明，任何伟大的自我克制会改变我们印钞的倾向。

:::

:::lang eng

2.“I expect a lot of inflation in the long run. Unfortunately, I can’t define ‘a lot’ and I can’t define ‘the long run’. But I expect worse inflation than we have ever seen before. Fortunately, I’ve been wrong \[about inflation\] quite often.”

:::

:::lang chs

2."从长远来看，我预计[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]会很严重。不幸的是，我无法明确'大量'，也无法明确'长期'。但我预计[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]会比我们以前看到的更严重。幸运的是，我经常出错。

:::

:::lang eng

-  [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] added ominously, “Most people in this room will see inflation that they have never seen before.” Happily, that did not turn out to be the case.

:::

:::lang chs

- [[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]严肃地补充道："在座的大多数人将会看到他们从未见过的[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]。令人欣慰的是，情况并非如此。

:::

:::lang eng

# On [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s acquisition strategy

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的[[Acquisitions|收购]]策略

:::

:::lang eng

3.“You can have a wonderful business without it being a wonderful stock. But it’s quite another story to have a wonderful stock without having a wonderful business.”

:::

:::lang chs

3."你可以拥有一家出色的企业，但这并不意味着它的股票也同样出色。然而，如果股票表现优异，却没有一家出色的企业作为支撑，那情况就完全不同了。“

:::

:::lang eng

4.“We have no equations. Ben [[Benjamin_Graham|Graham]] used to say that it’s a lot like selecting a wife. You can thoughtfully establish certain qualities you’d like her to have — and then, all of a sudden, you meet someone and you do it.”

:::

:::lang chs

4.“我们没有公式 本-[[Benjamin_Graham|格雷厄姆]]曾经说过，这就像选老婆一样。你可以深思熟虑地确定你希望她具备的某些品质--然后，突然间，你遇到了一个人，你就选了。”

:::

:::lang eng

- If [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] found a company that it wished to “marry” — at a fair valuation — [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] assured [[股东|shareholders]] that he would not be put off by a high price tag. “Don’t hold back on showing us a \[potential\] deal because we don’t have the money. We have yet to not do a deal because of not having enough money.”

:::

:::lang chs

- 如果[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]发现了一家它希望以合理估值 “[[Long_term_Investing|长期持有]]”的公司，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]向股东保证，他不会被高价所吓倒。"不要因为我们没钱，就不向我们展示一笔潜在交易。我们还没有因为资金不足而放弃交易。"

:::

:::lang eng

# On investing in Capital Cities/ABC

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于投资首都企业/美国广播公司

:::

:::lang eng

5.“Capital Cities is not a stock holding, but a 10-20 year relationship.”

:::

:::lang chs

5."首都企业/美国广播公司不是一时的股票持有，而是一种 10-20 年的合作关系。

:::

:::lang eng

- In 1985, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] helped finance the media company’s merger with ABC by purchasing 3 million shares of Cap Cities at $172.50 a piece. This $517 million investment made [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] the largest shareholder of Cap Cities/ABC.

:::

:::lang chs

- 1985 年，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司以每股 172.50 美元的价格购买了 300 万股首都企业的股票，为该媒体公司与美国广播公司的合并提供了资金支持。这笔 5.17 亿美元的投资使[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]成为首都企业/美国广播公司的最大股东。

:::

:::lang eng

6.“They have strong management at Cap Cities — the best management of any public company in the country. I have enormous confidence in Cap Cities.”

:::

:::lang chs

6."首都企业拥有强大的[[Management|管理层]]，是全国所有上市公司中管理最好的。我对首都企业非常有信心。

:::

:::lang eng

- This was no idle flattery. [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] had complete faith in the management duo of Tom Murphy and Dan Burke — even agreeing to sales restrictions on [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s stake and to vote his shares in accordance with Murphy and Burke.

:::

:::lang chs

- 这不是空洞的奉承。[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]对汤姆-墨菲和丹-伯克这对管理组合充满信心--甚至同意对[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的股份进行销售限制，并让墨菲和伯克来行使他的股份投票权。

:::

:::lang eng

7.“The near-term outlook \[for Cap Cities/ABC\] is not good. The value of ABC’s franchise has deteriorated in the last 6-12 months. However, the networks did $6.5 billion of business last year. It will be there — like radio. Despite inroads from television, \[radio\] is still quite profitable. ABC will be around forever.”

:::

:::lang chs

7."[首都企业/美国广播公司的]近期前景不容乐观。在过去的 6-12 个月里，美国广播公司的[[Franchise_Value|特许经营权]]价值已经下降。但是，电视网去年的业务量达到了 65 亿美元。它将继续存在--就像广播一样。尽管受到了电视的冲击，但广播仍然相当有利可图。美国广播公司将永远存续。"

:::

:::lang eng

- “If people ever take up reading,” [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] joked, “that will cut into \[our\] business.”

:::

:::lang chs

- “如果人们开始养成阅读习惯，”[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]开玩笑说 “那将会削减我们的业务”

:::

:::lang eng

8.“I have repressed the memory of the sale of Capital Cities \[a few years earlier\].”

:::

:::lang chs

8.“我已经淡忘了几年前出售首都企业的记忆”

:::

:::lang eng

- [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] had previously owned shares of Cap Cities, but sold out of this position in 1978-1980 at just $43 per share. And while [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] claimed to have repressed any memory of this blunder, [[Charlie_Munger|Munger]] was quick to point out: “I kept my personal holdings \[in Cap Cities\].”

:::

:::lang chs

- [[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]曾拥有首都企业的股份，但在 1978-1980 年间以每股 43 美元的价格卖出了。虽然[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]声称已经淡忘了这一失误，但[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]很快就指出："我保留了我个人的[首都企业]持股”

:::

:::lang eng

- Interestingly enough, OIDreported that [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s equity portfolio (which, presumably, the Cap Cities proceeds were reinvested into) actually outperformed the Cap Cities stock price between 1980 and 1985. So, ultimately, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] out-compounded his own mistake.

:::

:::lang chs

- 有趣的是，OID （杰出投资者文摘）报道称，在 1980 年至 1985 年期间，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的股票投资组合（据推测，首都企业的收益被重新投进去了）的表现实际上超过了首都企业的股价。因此，最终[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]还是错上加错。

:::

:::lang eng

# On speculative excess in media stocks

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于媒体股票的投机性过剩

:::

:::lang eng

9.“The underlying business has deteriorated substantially from competition, cable, the VCR, etc. — all adding up to more consumer alternatives. At the same time, media prices have skyrocketed. Prices don’t make sense to me.”

:::

:::lang chs

9."由于竞争、有线电视、录像机等因素的影响，基本业务已经严重恶化，所有这些都为消费者提供了更多的选择。与此同时，媒体的价格也在飞涨。价格对我来说没有意义。

:::

:::lang eng

10.“You don’t care what channel the football game comes in on. You don’t have loyalty to any \[one\] channel.”

:::

:::lang chs

10."你不在乎橄榄球赛在哪个频道播出。你对任何频道都没有忠诚度。

:::

:::lang eng

11.“I try not to get caught up in the current mania, but it’s hard to avoid.”

:::

:::lang chs

11.“我试着不被当前的狂热所迷惑，但这很难避免。”

:::

:::lang eng

12.“During tulip mania, single tulip bulbs were exchanged for houses and businesses. If you could buy a company that owned twelve tulip bulbs for a 20% discount to the value of those tulip bulbs, would that be a bargain?”

:::

:::lang chs

12."在郁金香狂热时期，单个郁金香球茎可以交换房屋和企业。如果你能以比这些郁金香球茎价值低 20% 的价格买下一家拥有 12 个郁金香球茎的公司，那会是一笔划算的交易吗？"。

:::

:::lang eng

- [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]’s point is that a 20% discount from an irrationally high price does not necessarily equal good value.

:::

:::lang chs

- [[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]的观点是，在不合理的高价基础上打八折并不一定等同于物美价廉。

:::

:::lang eng

- The _Omaha World-Herald_ gives a slightly different version of this quote (or maybe it’s a subsequent remark that’s just very similar to this one). Like I said, piecing this all together is a bit of a guessing game. The _OWH_ quote is below as #14.

13.“\[Tulip mania\] was mass delusion. People traded homes for a single tulip bulb. If you owned twelve tulips, is that \[the same thing as\] a sound company?”

:::

:::lang chs

13\. “郁金香狂热是一种群众性癫狂。人们用房子换一个郁金香球茎。如果你拥有十二朵郁金香，这和拥有一家健全的公司是[一回事]吗？”

:::lang eng

14.“Buyers are most silly when they’re most happy.”

:::

:::lang chs

In 1958, an estimated 80-100 million hula hoops were sold — but then quickly went out of fashion. “It was born in January and dead as a doornail by October,” said Rich Knerr of WHAM-O. By 1985, sales had stabilized in the 1-2 million range.

# On shorting stocks

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于做空股票

:::

:::lang eng

16.“The mathematics \[of shorting\] are very unattractive. You can go broke very quickly. If you had shorted tulip bulbs long after the prices had become silly, the prices would have kept going up for a long time. It’s a tough way to make a living. I’ve tried it.”

:::

:::lang chs

16."做空的数学原理是非常不吸引人的。你可能会很快破产。如果你在郁金香价格已经变得荒谬之后做空郁金香球茎，价格可能会在很长一段时间内继续上涨。这是一种艰难的谋生方式。我试过。“

:::

:::lang chs

17."（这）和买便宜的股票形成了鲜明的对比。只要你没有用[[Leverage|杠杆]]交易，你就可以在价格下跌的时候继续大量地买。"

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于拒绝出售子公司--哪怕是一个平庸的子公司

:::

:::lang eng

21.“You may quit having children if you keep having clunkers, but you don’t just cast them out.”

:::

:::lang chs

21."如果你一直生不出好孩子，你可以放弃生孩子，但你不能就这样把他们扔掉。

:::

:::lang eng

- [[Charlie_Munger|Munger]] jumped in to add that whatever this policy lacks in terms of bottom-line sense, it’s more than made up for by burnishing [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s reputation among sellers. “It allows \[us\] to see businesses for sale that \[we\] might not otherwise see.”

:::

:::lang chs

- [[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]插话补充说，尽管这项政策在直接的经济利益方面有所欠缺，但它通过提升[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]在卖家中的声誉而得到了充分的补偿。“这让我们能够看到一些可能本来看不到的待售企业。”

:::lang chs

22.“We don’t understand overseas businesses.”

:::

:::lang chs

:::

:::lang eng

# On [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s stock price

:::

:::lang chs

# 关于[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的股价

:::

:::lang eng

24.“The current market price \[around $2,690 per share\] is near the high end of [[Intrinsic_Value|intrinsic value]] — give or take 15%. But it’s not a crazy price, by any means.

:::

:::lang chs

24."目前的市场价格（大约每股 2,690 美元）接近[[Intrinsic_Value|内在价值]]的上限--上下浮动 15%。但这绝不是一个疯狂的价格。
