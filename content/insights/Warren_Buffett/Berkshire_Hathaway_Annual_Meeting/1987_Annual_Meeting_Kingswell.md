尽管如此，我今天还是把我能找到的有关[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]] 1987 年年度股东大会的所有资料整理出来。
:::

:::
:::lang chs
这当然不能说是对 1987 年会议的最终定论。毋庸置疑，其中定有诸多疏漏，但无奈时光机尚未问世，我已尽我所能，汇集了那一天所有可得的信息。

我非常希望这能对[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的学生们有所帮助。

:::lang eng

On May 19, 1987, approximately 500 [[股东|shareholders]] filed into the Witherspoon Concert Hall at Omaha’s Joslyn Art Museum for the [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] annual meeting.

:::

:::lang chs

1987 年 5 月 19 日，约 500 名股东来到奥马哈乔斯林艺术博物馆的威瑟斯彭音乐厅，参加[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的年会。

:::

:::lang eng

That Tuesday morning, [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] and [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie Munger]] sped through the formal proceedings in a matter of minutes — before opening the floor to questions from [[股东|shareholders]] for the next two to three hours.

:::

:::lang chs

周二上午，沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]和查理-[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]在短短几分钟内完成了正式流程--然后在接下来的两三个小时里接受股东提问。

:::

:::lang eng

The _Omaha World-Herald_ noted that “[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] constantly made humorous remarks while consuming Cherry [[Coca_Cola|Coke]]” — which, I must admit, is a pretty apt description of these Q&A sessions.

:::

:::lang eng

《

:::

:::lang chs

奥马哈世界先驱报》（Omaha World-Herald）指出，"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]一边喝着樱桃可乐，一边不断发表幽默言论"--我必须承认，用这句话来形容这些问答环节再恰当不过了。

:::

:::lang eng

Well, without further ado, here’s everything that I could dig up from this (very) early episode of the Warren & [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie]] show…

:::

:::lang chs

好了，废话不多说，下面是我能从这期（非常）早期的沃伦与查理问答中找到的所有内容...

:::

:::lang eng

**(1) Will [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] ever split its stock?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(1) [[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]是否会拆分股票？**

:::

:::lang eng

“I have a stockholder friend of mine who is 60,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]. “I just sent him a telegram on his birthday that said, ‘May you live until [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] splits.’”

:::

:::lang chs

"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："我有一位 60 岁的股东朋友。"我刚在他生日那天给他发了一封电报，上面写着'祝你活到[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]拆股那天'。

:::

:::lang eng

The implication being, of course, that it never will.

:::

:::lang chs

当然，这意味着它永远不会拆股。

:::

:::lang eng

If only immortality could be won so easily.

:::

:::lang chs

如果长生不老可以如此轻易地赢得，那该多好。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]’s reluctance to split the stock was the same then as it is today. [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] already has the exact group of patient, long-term [[股东|shareholders]] that he wants — and it’s “a hard group to upgrade”.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]当时不愿拆分股票的原因和今天一样。[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]已经拥有了他想要的那批耐心的长期股东--而且这批股东 "很难替代"。

:::

:::lang eng

**(2) Would splitting the stock help [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] get listed on the New York Stock Exchange?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(2) 拆分股票是否有助于[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]在纽约证券交易所上市？**

:::

:::lang eng

Back in 1987, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s stock was traded on the over-the-counter market. At least in part because its unusually large share price (already north of $3,000) all but ruled out inclusion in the NYSE.

:::

:::lang chs

1987 年，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的股票在场外市场交易。至少在一定程度上是因为其异常高的股价（已经超过 3000 美元）几乎排除了在纽约证券交易所上市的可能性。

:::

:::lang eng

“A couple of people from the New York Stock Exchange came out three or four months ago \[to try to convince me to list on the NYSE\],” [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] told [[股东|shareholders]].

:::

:::lang chs

"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]对股东们说："三四个月前，纽约证券交易所的几个人来（试图说服我在纽约证券交易所上市）。

:::

:::lang eng

In those days, stock was typically purchased in 100-share “round” lots — which would be prohibitively expensive at [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s lofty price. And, if someone wished to buy a smaller amount (known as an “odd” lot), they got nickel-and-dimed with extra fees.

:::

:::lang chs

在当时，股票通常以 100 股 "一手"的方式购买--以[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的高价来说，这将是非常昂贵的。而且，如果有人希望购买较小数量的股票（称为 "非整数"手），就会被收取额外的费用。

:::

:::lang eng

So [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] asked the NYSE to allow [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] to sell 10-share round lots as a compromise. “The New York Stock Exchange wants us,” he said, “but not the 10-share round lot. It wouldn’t work as 100 share trades.”

:::

:::lang chs

因此，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]要求纽约证券交易所允许[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]能以 10 股为一手出售股票，作为一种妥协。"他说："纽约证券交易所想要我们加入，但不接受 10 股为一手。它不能像 100 股为一手交易那样运作"。

:::

:::lang eng

He sounded like he preferred the sedate pace of OTC anyway. “The less stock traded,” he said, “the better job we’ve done. That’s not an attitude the NYSE likes.”

:::

:::lang chs

无论如何，他听起来更喜欢场外交易的缓慢节奏。他说："交易的股票越少，我们就做得越好。纽约证券交易所可不喜欢这种态度。"

:::

:::lang eng

-   The New York Stock Exchange agreed to [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s terms the following year and the Omaha-based conglomerate officially joined the Big Board on November 29, 1988 — with 10-share round lots instead of 100.

:::

:::lang chs

- 次年，纽约证券交易所同意了[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的条件，这家总部位于奥马哈的企业集团于 1988 年 11 月 29 日正式加入大证券交易所--以 10 股而不是 100 股为一手的交易方式上市。

:::

:::lang eng

**(3) Are there advantages to spinning off parts of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(3) 分拆[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的部分业务是否有好处？**

:::

:::lang eng

“I don’t think it’s easier to administer two public companies instead of one,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]].

:::

:::lang chs

"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："我不认为管理两家上市公司比管理一家公司更容易。

:::

:::lang eng

**(4) Will you repurchase shares to boost [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s value?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(4) 你会[[Share_Buybacks|回购]]股票来提升[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的价值吗？**

:::

:::lang eng

“It would have to be significantly discounted from its [[Intrinsic_Value|intrinsic value]],” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]. “[[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s stock trades close to its [[Intrinsic_Value|intrinsic value]] \[right now\].”

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："那必须大幅低于其[[Intrinsic_Value|内在价值]]。"[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的股票交易接近其[[Intrinsic_Value|内在价值]]"。

:::

:::lang eng

If that were to change, he said, “we’d repurchase some stock — but we don’t want that to happen.”

:::

:::lang chs

他说，如果这种情况发生变化，"我们会[[Share_Buybacks|回购]]一些股票--但我们不希望这种情况发生"。

:::

:::lang eng

**(5) Did your advertisement in the** _**Wall Street Journal**_ **last year lead to any deals?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(5) 你去年在《华尔街日报》上刊登的广告是否促成了交易？**

:::

:::lang eng

In 1986, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] placed an ad in the _Wall Street Journal_ in search of companies to acquire — because tax changes coming at the end of the year made it a particularly advantageous time for owners to sell. He sought businesses worth at least $100 million and with $10 million of after-tax earnings.

:::

:::lang chs

1986 年，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]在《华尔街日报》上刊登了一则广告，寻找可以[[Acquisitions|收购]]的公司--因为年底的税制改革使公司所有者在这个时候出售公司特别有利。他寻找的是价值至少 1 亿美元、税后盈利 1 千万美元的企业。

:::

:::lang eng

And while he got a lot of responses, the vast majority were not a fit. “If you run an ad for a chihuahua,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]], “you get a lot of collie replies.”

:::

:::lang chs

虽然他收到了很多回复，但绝大多数都不合适。"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："如果你刊登一则招聘吉娃娃的广告，你会收到很多牧羊犬的回复。

:::

:::lang eng

(An example of a “collie reply” was the Mississippi woman who tried to sell [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] her antebellum mansion. Or the man who wanted to offload his NYC newsstand.)

:::

:::lang chs

("牧羊犬回复 "的一个例子是，密西西比州的一位妇女试图把她的战前豪宅卖给[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]。或者是那个想卖掉纽约报摊的男人）。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] admitted that about five of the responses intrigued him, though none led to a deal. Nevertheless, he hoped that the advertisement made an impression on would-be sellers and might pay off down the line.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]承认，大约有五条回复引起了他的兴趣，但没有一条促成交易。尽管如此，他还是希望这则广告能给潜在卖家留下深刻印象，并在日后获得回报。

:::

:::lang eng

“The ad cost $47,000,” he said. “The _Wall Street Journal_ is expensive. Even though it didn’t work, people will remember the ad.”

:::

:::lang chs

他说："这则广告花费了 4.7 万美元。"《华尔街日报》很贵。即使没有成功，人们也会记住这个广告。

:::

:::lang eng

Until then, though, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] would go back to business as usual. “We have a sophisticated acquisition policy,” [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] said. “We wait for the phone to ring.”

:::

:::lang chs

不过，在此之前，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]将一切照旧。"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："我们有一套成熟的[[Acquisitions|收购]]政策。"我们等着电话铃响。"

:::

:::lang eng

**(6) What kind of businesses are you looking for?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(6) 你在寻找什么样的生意？**

:::

:::lang eng

“I’d like to buy something for a penny, sell it for a dollar, and have it be habit-forming,” he laughed.

:::

:::lang chs

他笑着说："我希望低价买，高价卖，而且能不断重复"。

:::

:::lang eng

Sadly, such unicorns are few and far between. “I don’t find any stocks that are attractive to me \[right now\].”

:::

:::lang chs

遗憾的是，这样的独角兽企业少之又少。"我找不到任何对我有吸引力的股票（现在）"。

:::

:::lang eng

**(7) Would you invest in a foreign corporation?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(7) 你会投资外国公司吗？**

:::

:::lang eng

“We had a few inquiries from Canada,” he said, “but it’s unlikely we’ll buy anything outside the United States.”

:::

:::lang chs

他说，"我们收到了一些来自加拿大的询价，但我们不太可能购买美国以外的任何东西"。

:::

:::lang eng

**(8) What’s going to happen with inflation?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(8) [[Inflation|通货膨胀]]会怎样？**

:::

:::lang eng

“At some point,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]], “we will see lots of inflation. We will not profit from it — and we’ll try not to be destroyed by it.”

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："在某些时候，我们会看到大量的[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]。我们不会从中获利--我们会尽量不被它摧毁。"

:::

:::lang eng

He believed that the U.S. government would continue to print more money as needed, thereby fueling further inflation. “If you had a printing press in your basement and you had some problems, you might slip down there occasionally \[too\],” he said.

:::

:::lang chs

他认为，美国政府将继续根据需要印制更多的钞票，从而进一步加剧[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]。他说："如果你的地下室里有一台印刷机，而你又遇到了一些问题，你可能会时不时的去印制钞票。

:::

:::lang eng

**(9) What is your position on protectionism?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(9) 你对保护主义的立场？**

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] referred the questioner to an op-ed that he wrote earlier that month for the _Washington Post_. In it, he proposed a “plan that will promptly eliminate the trade deficit but leave us free of most of the problems that quotas and tariffs produce”.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]向提问者提到了他当月早些时候为《[[Washington_Post|华盛顿邮报]]》撰写的一篇专栏文章。在这篇文章中，他提出了一个 "既能迅速消除贸易赤字，又能使我们摆脱配额和关税所带来的大多数问题的计划"。

:::

:::lang eng

His plan called for the creation and use of Import Certificates that would be issued to exporters based on the value of their exports. And any company seeking to import goods from overseas would need to purchase an equal amount of these ICs from the exporters. Voila, a trade balance.

:::

:::lang chs

他的计划要求创建和使用进口证书，根据出口商的出口价值向其发放。任何想要从海外进口商品的公司都需要从出口商那里购买等额的进口证书。这样，贸易就平衡了。

:::

:::lang eng

“In 1626, we made one of our better moves,” he wrote. “Encountering some consumption-oriented locals, we swapped a few trinkets for ownership of Manhattan Island. Now, we’ve begun reversing the process. We should stop this foolishness promptly — and ICs will do the job.”

:::

:::lang chs

他写道："1626 年，我们采取了一个更好的举措。遇到一些注重消费的当地人，我们用一些小玩意换取了曼哈顿岛的所有权。现在，我们开始采取不一样的措施。我们应该及时制止这种愚蠢的行为--进口证书将起到作用。"

:::

:::lang eng

**(10) What are your views on the insurance business?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(10) 您对[[Insurance_Industry|保险业]]务有何看法？**

:::

:::lang eng

In 1986, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s insurance operations achieved a combined ratio of 103% — pretty good for that era’s high interest rate environment — but there were ominous signs that increased capacity throughout the industry would soon drive down prices.

:::

:::lang chs

1986 年，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的[[Insurance_Industry|保险业]]务综合比率达到 103%，这在当时的高利率环境下相当不错，但有不祥之兆表明，整个行业产能的增加很快就会导致价格下降。

:::

:::lang eng

And that’s exactly what happened.

:::

:::lang chs

事实也正是如此。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s insurance volume dropped by 35% in the first quarter of 1987 — and [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] expected the situation to worsen in the coming years. “The \[insurance\] business is becoming intrinsically less profitable,” he said.

:::

:::lang chs

1987 年第一季度，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司的保险额下降了 35%，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]预计未来几年情况还会恶化。他说："从本质上讲，[[Insurance_Industry|保险业]]务的利润越来越低。"

:::

:::lang eng

**(11) Do you think executive compensation at [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] is too high?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(11)你认为[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的高管薪酬是否过高？**

:::

:::lang eng

This question seemed mostly aimed at insurance chief Mike Goldberg’s $2.2 million total compensation (salary and bonus) in 1986.

:::

:::lang chs

这个问题似乎主要针对[[Insurance_Industry|保险业]]主管迈克-戈德伯格 1986 年 220 万美元的总薪酬（工资和奖金）。

:::

:::lang eng

“Mike’s compensation relates to the business under his control,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]. “I pulled it down — and he pulled it up. Our compensation reflects that.” (A joke at the sizable difference between Goldberg’s multi-million-dollar pay package and [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]’s customary $100,000 salary.)

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："迈克的薪酬与他管理的业务有关。我的降低了，他的提高了。我们的报酬情况反映了这一点。(这是对戈德伯格数百万美元的薪酬待遇与[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]惯常的 10 万美元薪水之间巨大差距的玩笑）。"

:::

:::lang eng

“Mike’s not the only one up there,” he added. “The figures can’t get too big as far as we’re concerned.”

:::

:::lang chs

他补充说："高的不只有迈克一个人。就我们而言，数额不可能太大"。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] has said elsewhere that he never minds writing a big check for outstanding performance. In fact, it’s the managers who muddle along in mediocrity — never earning lucrative bonuses — that really bother him.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]曾在其他地方说过，他从来不介意为出色的业绩付高报酬。事实上，真正让他感到困扰的是那些平庸无为、从来没有获得过丰厚奖金的经理人。

:::

:::lang eng

**(12) What is the long-term plan for [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(12) [[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司的长期计划是什么？**

:::

:::lang eng

“We have no interest in selling or merging,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]. “We have no end-game strategy. [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie]] and I have fun. We like the people we work with.”

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："我们对出售或合并没有兴趣。我们没有终极战略。查理和我玩得很开心。我们喜欢与我们共事的人。"

:::

:::lang eng

**(13) What do you think will happen in the stock market?**

:::

:::lang chs

**(13) 你认为股市会发生什么变化？**

:::

:::lang eng

By 1987, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s stock cupboard looked pretty bare. [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] and [[Charlie_Munger|Munger]] had sold almost all of their non-permanent holdings because of the frothy market and invested the proceeds in tax-free bonds.

:::

:::lang chs

到 1987 年，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]的股票投资选择已经相当有限。面对泡沫化的市场，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]和[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]几乎卖掉了所有非长期持股，并将收益投资于免税[[Bonds|债券]]。

:::

:::lang eng

It was a time of all greed and no fear on Wall Street — which has never been [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s kind of market. Killer timing, too, with the Black Monday crash just a few months away.

:::

:::lang chs

当时的华尔街只有贪婪，没有恐惧--[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]从来不喜欢这样的市场。[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]选择的时机也很好，黑色星期一崩盘就在几个月后。
