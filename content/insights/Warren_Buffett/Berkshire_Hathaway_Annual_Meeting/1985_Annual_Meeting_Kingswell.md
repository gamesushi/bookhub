#  再现[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]·哈撒韦公司 1985 年年会

:::

:::lang eng

There’s just one drawback: CNBC’s archive of these [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] Q&A sessions only dates back to 1994. _The University of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] Hathaway_, written by Daniel Pecaut and Corey Wrenn, fills in some of the gaps — but only back to 1986.

:::

:::lang chs

有个缺憾： CNBC 的[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]问答会档案只能追溯到 1994 年。丹尼尔-佩科特和科里-沃伦撰写的《波克夏大學》填补了部分空白，但只追溯到 1986 年。

:::

:::lang eng

As such, I feared that any comprehensive accounts of earlier annual meetings had been irrevocably lost in the mists of time.

:::

:::lang chs

因此，我担心关于早期年会的任何全面记录都已在时间的迷雾中不可挽回地消失了。

:::

:::lang eng

But, happily, I was wrong.

:::

:::lang chs

但令人欣慰的是，我错了。

:::

:::lang eng

I recently stumbled upon an _Omaha World-Herald_ story written about [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s 1985 annual meeting. It’s certainly not perfect — some of [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]]’s answers are paraphrased by the author rather than quoting him directly — but it’s the most information about this “lost” annual meeting that I’ve ever seen gathered together in one place. Written by someone who was actually there.

:::

:::lang chs

最近，我偶然发现了一篇《奥马哈世界先驱报》关于[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司 1985 年年会的报道。这篇报道当然并不完美--沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]的一些回答是作者转述的，而不是直接引用他的话--但这是我所见过的关于这次 "失落 "的年会的最多信息。由亲历者撰写。

:::

:::lang eng

In the mid-1980s, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]’s annual meeting was an entirely different beast than today’s weekend-long “Woodstock for Capitalists”. Attendees didn’t have to book their hotel rooms months in advance or wake up before dawn just to get in line outside of an arena. There was no mad rush for seats once the doors opened.

:::

:::lang chs

20 世纪 80 年代中期，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司的年会与今天的 "资本家的伍德斯托克音乐节 "完全不同。与会者不必提前几个月预订酒店房间，也不必为了在会场外排队而在黎明前起床。门一打开，也不会出现疯狂抢座位的现象。

:::

:::lang eng

It was a quieter, simpler chapter in [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s history.

:::

:::lang chs

这是[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]历史上更安静、更简单的篇章。

:::

:::lang eng

So quiet, in fact, that 1985’s annual meeting was held on a Tuesday. And, instead of a cavernous arena, [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] and [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie Munger]] opted for the Red Lion Inn in downtown Omaha. Approximately 250 [[股东|shareholders]] attended the meeting and the ensuing Q&A session lasted only — only? — two hours.

:::

:::lang chs

事实上，1985 年的年会是在星期二举行的，非常低调。沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]和查理-[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]选择了奥马哈市中心的红狮酒店，而不是一个巨大的会场。大约 250 名股东出席了会议，随后的问答环节只持续了两个小时。

:::

:::lang eng

While 250 attendees might sound laughably tiny by current standards, the growing crowd spurred [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] to move future meetings to a more spacious auditorium in Omaha’s Joslyn Art Museum. Just five years earlier, in 1980, the headcount was thirty.

:::

:::lang chs

虽然以现在的标准来衡量，250 名与会者听起来可能少得可笑，但越来越多的人促使[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]将以后的会议搬到了奥马哈乔斯林艺术博物馆更宽敞的礼堂。而就在五年前的 1980 年，会议人数只有 30 人。

:::

:::lang eng

The most shocking difference, though, had nothing to do with location or attendance. As [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] fielded questions from [[股东|shareholders]] that day, he sipped on a Pepsi (which, back then, was still his beverage of choice).

:::

:::lang chs

不过，最令人震惊的不同之处与会议地点或出席人数无关。那天，当[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]回答股东们的问题时，他喝着百事可乐（当时，百事可乐仍是他的首选饮料）。

:::

:::lang eng

Some things, though, never change:

:::

:::lang chs

不过，有些事情永远不会改变：

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] drew laughter with his quick wit. When [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] secretary Verne McKenzie tested his microphone with “one, two, three”, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] quickly spoke into his microphone: “One million, two million, three million.”

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]的机智引来了阵阵笑声。当[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司秘书维恩-麦肯齐（Verne McKenzie）用 "一、二、三 "测试麦克风时，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]迅速对着麦克风说了起来： "一百万 二百万 三百万"

:::

:::lang eng

Here are some of the highlights from this (very) early episode of the Warren & [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie]] Show…

:::

:::lang chs

以下是《沃伦与查理秀》早期节目的部分精彩片段...

:::

:::lang eng

## HOW TO VALUE A BUSINESS

:::

:::lang chs

## 如何为企业估值

:::

:::lang eng

“Do a lot of reading,” replied [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]].

:::

:::lang chs

"多读书，"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]回答道。

:::

:::lang eng

Generally speaking, he recommended the writings of [[Benjamin_Graham|Benjamin Graham]] and Philip Fisher for those trying to sharpen their investment mindset — and annual reports and trade magazines for evaluating particular businesses and industries.

:::

:::lang chs

一般来说，他推荐那些试图提高投资思维的人阅读本杰明-[[Benjamin_Graham|格雷厄姆]]（[[Benjamin_Graham|Benjamin Graham]]）和菲利普-费雪（Philip Fisher）的著作，推荐年度报告和行业杂志来评估特定企业和行业。

:::

:::lang eng

Reading, he insisted, is more important than speaking with company executives or other investors. In fact, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] admitted that he had recently purchased a substantial amount of Exxon stock before talking to any of that company’s executives. “You’re not going to get any brilliant insights walking into the \[Exxon\] building,” he said.

:::

:::lang chs

他坚持认为，阅读比与公司高管或其他投资者交谈更重要。事实上，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]承认，他最近在与埃克森公司的任何一位高管交谈之前，就购买了大量该公司的股票。"他说："走进埃克森公司的大楼，你不会得到任何真知灼见。

:::

:::lang eng

And, at least in the money game, size matters.

:::

:::lang chs

至少在金钱游戏中，规模很重要。

:::

:::lang eng

It’s easier to determine the value of a large business than a small one, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] said. If someone buys a gas station, for example, another station opening across the street could have a major effect on the value of the first station.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说，确定大企业的价值比确定小企业的价值要容易得多。例如，如果有人买下了一家加油站，那么街对面开张的另一家加油站可能会对第一家加油站的价值产生重大影响。

:::

:::lang eng

## DIVIDEND DILEMMA

:::

:::lang chs

## [[Dividends|股息]]窘境

:::

:::lang eng

In 1985, it had been nearly twenty years since [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] last paid a [[Dividends|dividend]]. The company (wisely) reasoned that [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] could reinvest this excess capital at higher rates of return than [[股东|shareholders]] could themselves, so any [[Dividends|dividend]] would ultimately prove counterproductive.

:::

:::lang chs

1985 年，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司已经将近 20 年没有派发[[Dividends|股息]]了。公司（明智地）推断，沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]可以比股东自身更高的回报率将这些多余的资本进行再投资，因此任何分红最终都会适得其反。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] actually polled its [[股东|shareholders]] on this subject and [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] announced the results at the annual meeting.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司实际上就这一问题对股东进行了民意调查，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]在年会上宣布了调查结果。

:::

:::lang eng

The company, which has 3,100 [[股东|shareholders]], received 2,022 responses.

:::

:::lang chs

这家拥有 3100 名股东的公司收到了 2022 份回复。

:::

:::lang eng

88% of those responding said they favored the company’s current policy of reinvesting earnings rather than paying [[Dividends|dividends]] to [[股东|shareholders]].

:::

:::lang chs

88%的受访者表示，他们赞成公司目前将盈利再投资而不是向股东分红的政策。

:::

:::lang eng

A similar percentage also approved of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]]’s policy towards charitable contributions — in which individual [[股东|shareholders]] designate which charity receives their share of the overall contribution.

:::

:::lang chs

类似比例的人还赞成[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司的慈善捐款政策，即由股东个人指定哪家慈善机构接收他们在整个捐款中所占的份额。

:::

:::lang eng

## WHAT ABOUT INFLATION?

:::

:::lang chs

## [[Inflation|通货膨胀]]问题

:::

:::lang eng

Being the mid-1980s, the scourge of inflation was understandably a hot topic — leading one shareholder to ask [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] for his prediction on where inflation would go in the future.

:::

:::lang chs

在 20 世纪 80 年代中期，[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]是一个热门话题，这一点是可以理解的，因此一位股东向[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]询问他对未来[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]的预测。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] conceded that there was a small chance — maybe 5% — of runaway inflation occurring at some point in the years ahead. “That shouldn’t happen,” he said, “but we can’t rule it out.”

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]承认，在未来几年的某个时候，[[Inflation|通货膨胀]]失控的可能性很小，可能只有 5%。他说："这种情况应该不会发生，""但我们不能排除这种可能性。"

:::

:::lang eng

Instead, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] predicted “moderate inflation” of about 7% as the most likely possibility. But, of course, he “stressed \[that\] he is reluctant to predict a precise inflation percentage”.

:::

:::lang chs

相反，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]预测最有可能出现的是 7% 左右的 "温和通胀"。当然，他 "强调他不愿意预测一个精确的通胀率"。

:::

:::lang eng

## THE POST-BUFFETT ERA

:::

:::lang chs

## 后[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]时代

:::

:::lang eng

If you listen to [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] [[股东|shareholders]] (or BPL partners before them), [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] has had one foot in the grave for decades. Almost no annual meeting goes by without a question (or two) about what will happen to [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] after [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] shuffles off this mortal coil.

:::

:::lang chs

如果你听[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司股东（或之前的 BPL 合伙人）说，几十年来沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]似乎随时可能离世。几乎没有一次年会不被问及[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]离开人世后，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司会发生什么。

:::

:::lang eng

“If something happened to me tomorrow,” [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] patiently replied in 1985, “[[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] will not be sold and parts of it will not be sold.”

:::

:::lang chs

"1985 年，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]耐心地回答说："如果明天我出了什么事，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]不会出售，那怕其中的一部分也不会出售。

:::

:::lang eng

And, although he didn’t explicitly outline his succession plan, he reassured [[股东|shareholders]] that [[Charlie_Munger|Charlie Munger]] would step into the breach in his absence. “[[Charlie_Munger|Charlie]] and I think alike,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]].

:::

:::lang chs

而且，尽管他没有明确概述自己的继任计划，但他向股东保证，查理-[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]将在他缺席时接任。"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："查理和我的想法一致。

:::

:::lang eng

Other than that, it would be business as usual for [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]].

:::

:::lang chs

除此之外，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司一切照旧。

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] said he doesn’t want to anticipate his departure by having unnecessary people in his corporate office. “With lots of help, we’d do worse,” he said.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说，他不想在公司办公室安排多余的人，以防他离开。"他说："如果有很多人帮忙，我们会做得更糟。

:::

:::lang eng

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION

:::

:::lang chs

## 资本分配

:::

:::lang eng

Does [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] prefer to buy whole businesses (like See’s Candies or Nebraska Furniture Mart) or blocks of common stock that represent partial ownership (like the _Washington Post_ or Capital Cities/ABC)?

:::

:::lang chs

沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]更喜欢购买整个企业（如 See's Candies 或 Nebraska Furniture Mart），还是购买代表部分所有权的普通股（如《[[Washington_Post|华盛顿邮报]]》或 Capital Cities/ABC）？

:::

:::lang eng

[[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]]: “We would really like a collection of whole businesses that meet our \[investment\] standards.”

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]："我们真的希望拥有一系列符合我们投资标准的整个企业。

:::

:::lang eng

However, he said, there have been more investment opportunities in marketable securities in recent months. “\[But\] there are not many opportunities in either area right now.”

:::

:::lang chs

不过，他说，最近几个月可交易证券的投资机会更多了。"[但是]现在这两个领域的机会都不多。"

:::

:::lang eng

## CAN BERKSHIRE KEEP UP THE PACE?

:::

:::lang chs

## [[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]能否保持增长速度？

:::

:::lang eng

In 1984, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]’s growth in net worth slowed to 13.6% — far below the 20+% annual growth that [[股东|shareholders]] had grown accustomed to over the previous two decades.

:::

:::lang chs

1984 年，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司的净资产增长率放缓至 13.6%，远远低于股东们在过去二十年里已经习惯的 20% 以上的年增长率。

:::

:::lang eng

This led [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] to remind everyone of a fact that he had been repeating over and over since his [[合伙基金|partnership]] days: [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire]] will not grow as fast in the future as it has in the past.

:::

:::lang chs

这促使沃伦-[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]提醒大家一个事实，这是他从合伙时代起就反复强调的： [[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]公司未来的增长速度不会像过去那样快。

:::

:::lang eng

(Of course, [[股东|shareholders]] can be forgiven for treating [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] a bit like The Boy Who Cried Wolf on this subject. He continually warned them about slowing returns dating all the way back to the 1960s, but then just kept on posting eye-popping gains year in and year out.)

:::

:::lang chs

(当然，在这个问题上，股东们可以原谅[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]有点像 "狼来了的男孩"。自 20 世纪 60 年代以来，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]就不断警告他们回报率会放缓，但却年复一年地获得令人瞠目的收益。）

:::

:::lang eng

Nevertheless, he once again cautioned the crowd to keep their expectations in check.

:::

:::lang chs

尽管如此，他还是再次告诫大家要控制好自己的预期。

:::

:::lang eng

“I can guarantee \[that\] we will not do as well as in the past,” said [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]].

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说："我可以保证，我们的业绩不会像过去那么好。"

:::

:::lang eng

There have been fewer undervalued companies and stocks to buy recently, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] said. He added that as his company has become larger, it has been harder to achieve the same percentage investment returns.

:::

:::lang chs

[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]说，最近被[[Undervalued|低估]]的公司和股票越来越少。他补充说，随着公司规模的扩大，要实现相同比例的投资回报也越来越难。

:::

:::lang eng

“I still think we may be able to do better than American industry as a whole.”

:::

:::lang chs

"我仍然认为，我们可能会比美国整个行业做得更好"。

:::

:::lang eng

And, on that last point, [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] was right.

:::

:::lang chs

最后一点，[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]是对的。

:::

:::lang eng

Since May 21, 1985, [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] has racked up a 15.9% annual return as compared to the [[标准普尔500指数|S&P 500]]’s 11.1% mark. Not bad for a company that’s slowing down.

:::

:::lang chs

自 1985 年 5 月 21 日以来，[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司的年回报率达到 15.9%，而标准普尔 500 指数只有 11.1%。对于一家增长放缓的公司来说，这已经不错了。

:::

:::lang eng

## A COUPLE OF LAUGHS & A ROUND OF APPLAUSE

:::

:::lang chs

## 轻松的笑声和热烈的掌声

:::

:::lang eng

No annual meeting is ever complete without some of that trademark [[Warren_Buffett|Warren Buffett]] wit.

:::

:::lang chs

没有[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]标志性的机智，年会是不完整的。

:::

:::lang eng

- Will the federal deficit be substantially reduced? “I’ll believe it when I see it.”

:::

:::lang chs

- 联邦赤字会大幅减少吗？"我看到了就会相信"。

:::

:::lang eng

- What about so-called “junk” bonds? “I think they’ll live up to their name,” [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] quipped.

:::

:::lang chs

- 所谓的 "垃圾 "[[Bonds|债券]]呢？"[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]调侃道："我认为它们会名副其实。

:::

:::lang eng

At the end of the 1985 annual meeting, one shareholder stood up with a word of thanks for Messrs. [[Warren_Buffett|Buffett]] and [[Charlie_Munger|Munger]]: “On behalf of my children \[who presumably will benefit from the increasing value of [[Berkshire_Hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]] stock\], I thank you two for working so hard.”

:::

:::lang chs

1985 年年会结束时，一位股东站起来对[[Warren_Buffett|巴菲特]]和[[Charlie_Munger|芒格]]先生说了一句感谢的话："我代表我的孩子们（他们大概会从[[Berkshire_Hathaway|伯克希尔]]-哈撒韦公司股票不断增值中获益），感谢你们两位的辛勤工作"。

:::

:::lang eng

The rest of the crowd then joined in with a round of applause for the two men at the front of the room who had created such generational wealth for so many of them.

:::

:::lang chs

随后，在场的其他人一起为前面的两位先生鼓掌，他们为许多人创造了世代相传的财富。

:::
