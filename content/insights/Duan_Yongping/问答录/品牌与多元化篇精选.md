# 品牌与多元化篇精选
# Selected Quotes on Brands & Diversification

---

## 💡 Core Golden Quotes

- "In most cases, I don't know how to prioritize quality without prioritizing brands"
- "The lower social trust becomes, the more important brands are"
- "Selling the same thing under multiple brands is clearly business-oriented, not user-oriented"
- "Very few companies can do 'diversification' well—especially those who diversify for diversification's sake"
- "I think Moutai should subtract rather than add"

---

## ❓ Selected Q&A

**Q**: Can you prioritize quality without prioritizing brands?

**A**: In most cases, I don't know how to prioritize quality without prioritizing brands. (2010-06-17)

---

**Q**: Do brands have value in the real estate industry?

**A**: Think about it—if you were buying a house yourself, would you care about the brand? I don't believe anyone wouldn't. (2010-07-05)

---

**Q**: What's the relationship between social trust and brands?

**A**: The lower social trust becomes, the more important brands are. (2010-07-08)

---

**Q**: What are the benefits of a multi-brand strategy?

**A**: For most products, doing this is very foolish. Years ago I saw P&G doing this, so I always felt their products had something slightly deceptive about them—someday they'd get into trouble. (2017-08-17)

---

**Q**: What orientation does selling the same thing under multiple brands represent?

**A**: Selling the same thing under multiple brands is clearly business-oriented, not user-oriented. (2017-08-20)

---

**Q**: If you can't even handle one brand well, can you handle multiple brands well?

**A**: If it's the same product and you can't handle one brand well, then handling multiple brands will be even worse. If you can handle one brand well, why would you need multiple brands? (2017-08-20)

---

**Q**: Should Moutai add or subtract?

**A**: I think [[Moutai]] should subtract rather than add. It seems like Moutai is also somewhat heading in this direction—I hope they persist. (Investment Logic Chapter)

---

**Q**: What's wrong with diversification?

**A**: Very few companies can do "diversification" well—especially those who diversify for diversification's sake. (2017-08-14)

---

**Q**: How should we understand "only famous enterprises get attention"?

**A**: Only famous enterprises get attention, and only enterprises that care about their own brand will care about it, and... get it? (2013-03-16)

---

## 💡 Duan Yongping's Brand Philosophy

1. **Brand as Carrier of Quality**: Without prioritizing brands, you cannot prioritize quality
2. **Focus on One Brand**: Doing one brand well is better than doing multiple brands poorly
3. **User-Oriented**: Brand strategy should be user-centered, not business-centered
4. **Subtraction**: Good companies should focus rather than diversify

## 💡 核心金句

- \"多数情况下，不知道在不重视品牌的前提下，如何重视质量\"
- \"社会信任度越低，品牌的作用越大\"
- \"同样的东西分成多个品牌卖显然属于生意导向而不是用户导向\"
- \"很少有公司能够做好'多元化'的，尤其是那些为了多元化而多元化的\"
- \"我认为茅台应该做减法而不是加法\"

---

## ❓ 精选问答

**问**：不重视品牌，可以重视质量吗？

**答**：多数情况下，不知道在不重视品牌的前提下，如何重视质量。（2010-06-17）

---

**问**：品牌在房地产行业有价值吗？

**答**：想想如果你自己买房子的话，你会在乎品牌吗？我不信有人不在乎。（2010-07-05）

---

**问**：社会信任度和品牌的关系是什么？

**答**：社会信任度越低，品牌的作用越大。（2010-07-08）

---

**问**：多品牌策略有哪些好处？

**答**：大部分产品这么做是非常愚蠢的。早年看过P&G这么做，所以老是觉得他们的产品多少有点蒙人的味道，将来有一天会有麻烦的。（2017-08-17）

---

**问**：同样的东西分成多个品牌卖，是什么导向？

**答**：同样的东西分成多个品牌卖显然属于生意导向而不是用户导向。（2017-08-20）

---

**问**：如果一个牌子都做不好，多个牌子能做好吗？

**答**：如果是同样的东西，一个牌子都做不好的话，多个牌子就更做不好了。如果一个牌子能做好的话，为什么还要多个牌子？（2017-08-20）

---

**问**：茅台应该做加法还是减法？

**答**：我认为[[茅台]]应该做减法而不是加法。好像茅台正在做的也有点这个意思，希望他们能坚持。（投资逻辑篇）

---

**问**：多元化有什么问题？

**答**：很少有公司能够做好\"多元化\"的，尤其是那些为了多元化而多元化的。（2017-08-14）

---

**问**：只有有名的企业才会有瞩目率，这句话怎么理解？

**答**：只有有名的企业才会有瞩目率，也只有在乎自己品牌的企业才会在乎，而且……明白？（2013-03-16）

---

## 💡 段永平的品牌哲学

1. **品牌是质量的载体**：不重视品牌，就无法重视质量
2. **专注一个品牌**：一个牌子做好，比多个牌子都做不好要强
3. **用户导向**：品牌策略要以用户为中心，而不是以生意为中心
4. **做减法**：好公司应该聚焦，而不是多元化
