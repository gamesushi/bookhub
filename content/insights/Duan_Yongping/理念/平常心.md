# Common Sense (Pingchangxin)

> **"All our success comes from 'Benfen + Common Sense'"**

---

## 📌 Concept Analysis

**Common Sense = Returning to the essence of things, viewing everything rationally, not being carried away by short-term fluctuations and emotions.**

Common Sense = rational = returning to the essence of things. When you have Common Sense, you ask: "Has this company's business gotten worse?" rather than "The stock price dropped — should I cut my losses?"

---

## 💡 Core Understanding

**1. Common Sense is rationality — returning to the essence of things.**

[[Benfen]] and Common Sense are core secrets of [[BBK]]'s [[Corporate Culture]].

**2. Common Sense is something already within you; it's only discovered through making mistakes and suffering losses.**

Common Sense isn't learned; it's gradually uncovered through mistakes and reflection.

**3. Common Sense is uncommon — ordinary people find it genuinely hard to achieve.**

This is why most people become emotional during stock price volatility — achieving true rationality is far harder than imagined.

**4. Common Sense is "clarity of mind" — the psychological foundation for doing right things.**

Duan cites the *Qingjing Jing* (Scripture of Clarity and Stillness): "When humans can maintain constant clarity, heaven and earth all return to them." Common Sense IS clarity of mind — what Buddhism calls supreme wisdom. "Only with Common Sense can one practice Benfen." Returning to clarity of mind and persisting in doing right things — that process IS Benfen. (2018-12-17, Business Logic Chapter)

**5. Professional investors need Common Sense most — don't be a professional investor without it.**

Common Sense is the fundamental for investors to remain rational amid market fluctuations. (2011-03-08, Investment Logic Chapter)

---

## ❓ Selected Q&A

**Q:** How do you understand the role of Common Sense in investing?

**A:** "Common Sense," simply put, means returning to the source of things. Knowing what "the source of things" means isn't easy. For example in investing, what you're buying is a company's future net cash flow — everything else is irrelevant. But figuring out a company's future cash flow is extremely difficult. When you can't figure out a company's future cash flow, what exactly are you buying? (Investment Logic Chapter)

---

**Q:** Why don't you recommend becoming a professional investor?

**A:** I've always believed if someone is still wrestling with these kinds of questions, they shouldn't become a professional investor — otherwise it'll be very hard to maintain Common Sense. My company doesn't generate any cash flow for me; I haven't needed it for ages. (2011-03-08, Investment Logic Chapter)

---

**Q:** What's the relationship between Common Sense and Benfen?

**A:** Common Sense essentially means at any time — especially when facing temptation — being able to block out all external interference, return to the essence of things (the origin), distinguish right from wrong, and know what constitutes doing right things. (2016-10-12, Business Logic Chapter)

---

## 📖 Case Study Breakdown

**Common Sense When NetEase Dropped Below $1**

When Duan bought NetEase in 2002, its stock had fallen from its peak to under $1 per share — market panic was everywhere. He says: "From when I started buying until three months after NetEase dropped below $1, my buy orders probably accounted for about half of all daily volume over those two-plus months." The ability to keep buying amid extreme panic IS precisely the embodiment of Common Sense — he focused on the company's future cash flows, not on stock price.

**Common Sense During the Maotai Plasticizer Scandal**

In 2012 during the Maotai plasticizer scandal, the stock price crashed. Duan bought Maotai at around ¥180 based on "right business + right people + right price." Amid market panic, people with Common Sense would ask: "Has Maotai's business model changed?" rather than "How much further will the price drop?"

---

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls

- ❌ **"Common Sense means numbness — indifferent to gains and losses"** — Common Sense IS "rational" — it doesn't mean not caring; it means caring about company fundamentals rather than stock price volatility. When prices drop, people with Common Sense ask "has the company's business worsened?" rather than panic-selling. (Source: Stanford 2018 speech)

- ❌ **"Common Sense is innate — some people just can't do it"** — "Maintaining it isn't hard because it's right there (it's something already inside you). Discovering it comes through making mistakes and suffering losses." Common Sense exists innately in everyone — it just needs mistakes and reflection to uncover it. (2018-09-30, Stanford SDL Dialogue)

- ❌ **"Having Common Sense qualifies you for professional investing"** — Common Sense is necessary but not sufficient. "If you're still thinking about how much steady cash flow you need, you'd better not become a 'professional investor'" — you also need the capability of truly understanding companies. (2011-03-08, Investment Logic Chapter)

# 平常心

> **"我们所有的成功，都来自于'本分+平常心'"**

---

## 📌 概念解析

**平常心 = 回到事物的本原，理性地看待一切，不被短期波动和情绪带走。**

平常心 = 理性（rational）= 回到事物的本原。当你有平常心的时候，你会问："这家公司的生意变差了吗？"而不是"股价跌了，我要不要止损？"

---

## 💡 核心理解

**1. 平常心就是 rational（理性），就是回到事物的本原。**

[[本分]]和平常心是[[步步高]][[企业文化]]的核心秘诀。

**2. 平常心是内心已有的东西，靠吃亏才能发现。**

平常心不是学来的，是通过犯错和反思慢慢发现的。

**3. 平常心是不平常的，普通人很难真正做到。**

这也是为什么大多数人在股价波动时会情绪化——做到真正的理性，比想象中难得多。

**4. 平常心就是"清净心"，是做对的事情的心理基础。**

段永平引用《清静经》："人能常清静，天地悉皆归"。平常心就是清净心，是佛家所说的无上智慧。"只有平常心才能本分。"回归清净心，做正确事情的这个过程的坚持，就是本分。（2018-12-17，商业逻辑篇）

**5. 职业投资者最需要平常心——没有平常心就不要做职业投资。**

平常心是投资者在市场波动中保持理性的根本。（2011-03-08，投资逻辑篇）

---

## ❓ 精选问答

**问**：如何理解平常心在投资中的作用？

**答**："平常心"简单讲就是回到事物的本源，知道什么是"事物的本源"不是件容易的事。比如投资，买的就是公司未来的净现金流，别的都是没关系的。但要搞明白公司未来的净现金流是件非常困难的事情。在搞不清公司未来现金流的前提下的投资买的到底是什么呢？（投资逻辑篇）

---

**问**：为什么不建议做职业投资者？

**答**：我反正一直认为如果谁还在为这些问题纠结的时候，就最好不要做职业投资，不然很难保持平常心。我们公司不给我产生任何现金流，我早就不需要了。（2011-03-08，投资逻辑篇）

---

**问**：平常心和本分是什么关系？

**答**：平常心其实就是在任何时候，尤其是在有诱惑的时候，能够排除所有外界的干扰，回到事物的本质（原点），辨别事情的是非与对错，知道什么是对的事情。（2016-10-12，商业逻辑篇）

---

## 📖 案例拆解

**网易跌破1块钱时的平常心**

段永平在2002年买入网易时，网易股价从最高点跌到不足1美元，市场一片恐慌。他"从开始买到网易跌破1块钱满3个月的那天总共两个多月的时间里，每天的买单可能有一半都是我的"。这种在极度恐慌中持续买入的能力，正是平常心的体现——他关注的不是股价，而是公司的未来现金流。

**茅台塑化剂事件时的平常心**

2012年茅台塑化剂事件，股价暴跌。段永平在约180元买入茅台，理由是"right business + right people + right price"。市场恐慌时，有平常心的人会问："茅台的生意模式变了吗？"而不是"股价还会跌多少？"

---

## ⚠️ 常见误区

- ❌ **"平常心就是麻木，对涨跌无所谓"** — 平常心就是"rational（理性）"——不是不关心，而是关心的是公司基本面，而不是股价波动。股价跌了，有平常心的人会问"公司的生意变差了吗"，而不是恐慌抛售。（来源：2018年斯坦福演讲）

- ❌ **"平常心是天生的，有些人就是做不到"** — "保持不难，因为它就在那儿（是内心已经有的东西）。发现嘛，靠吃亏。"平常心是每个人内心本有的，只是需要通过犯错和反思才能发现。（2018-09-30，斯坦福SDL对话）

- ❌ **"有了平常心就可以做职业投资"** — 平常心是必要条件，但不是充分条件。"如果你还在想需要多少稳定现金流的时候，最好还是不要做'职业投资者'"——还需要真正看懂公司的能力。（2011-03-08，投资逻辑篇）
